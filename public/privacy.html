DUEL Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

DUEL is a photo voting and ranking app. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

Account information: your email address and display name. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. If you sign up with a password, it is stored in hashed form and we cannot read it.

Content you provide: photos you upload, their titles, and your votes.

Technical data: basic server logs such as IP address and request time, used for security and troubleshooting.

How we use it

We use this information to run your account, show photos for voting, calculate rankings and leaderboards, keep the service secure, and respond to support requests. We do not sell your personal information and we do not show ads.

Services we use

DUEL relies on third-party providers that process data on our behalf: Cloudflare (website hosting), Render (application server), Turso (database), Cloudinary (photo storage) and Google (sign-in).

Public content

Photos you upload and your display name may be visible to other users, including on the leaderboard. Do not upload anything you do not want others to see.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and uploaded photos at any time by emailing us. We will act on verified requests within a reasonable time.

Children

DUEL is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
